## ◆ fantasy-worlds  _(source: arjun988)_

> Fantasy world-building art direction for Blender covering medieval, high fantasy, dark fantasy, and urban fantasy asset language.

## Fantasy Worlds

Pick fantasy subgenre and keep material language consistent.

### Subgenres
Medieval low-magic · High fantasy · Dark fantasy · Urban fantasy

### Tenets
1. Material honesty (wood/stone/leather/magic emissive rules)
2. Silhouette heraldry readable
3. Magic FX gated by `vfx-fx` (→ guides/12-simulation-vfx-weather.md)
4. Architecture matches tech level

## ◆ cozy-wholesome-mood  _(source: arjun988)_

> Cozy, cute, wholesome, relaxing, and peaceful mood direction for Blender assets and scenes.

## Cozy / Wholesome Mood

Warm light. Soft shapes. Safe clutter. Low threat.

### Tenets
1. Warm keys / soft fill
2. Rounded prop language
3. Clutter = comfort, not hazard
4. Gentle palettes
5. Cameras at friendly eye height
